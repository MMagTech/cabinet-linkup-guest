# Every screen is the console's LinkUp

- **Decided:** each screen looks and reads as LinkUp does on the console. Its numbers come from the
  console's code, and its words from the console's own `linkuprules.cpp`, built from the `cabinetos/`
  submodule, never copied. MMagTech judges every screen in the gallery (`2pstart-gallery`) before
  anything is built on it.
- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Why:** a friend should feel they are at the host's console. One copy of the words means a change
  on the console reaches 2P Start when the submodule moves.
- **Numbers:** #2, "The screens, as a gallery to judge before anything is built on them".
- **Replaces:** none.
